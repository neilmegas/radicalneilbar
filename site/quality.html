Confidence dimensions

These labels are displayed on every full evidence card. They are descriptive checks, not probabilities.

Source Identity
Whether the publisher is a verified party, parliament, named news outlet, or unresolved source.
Date
Whether a publication date is recorded for the source.
Attribution
Whether the act or quotation is directly attributable to the party or a named actor.
Translation
Whether English text is unnecessary, model-translated, round-trip checked, or flagged.
Relevance
The analysis-stage confidence that the record meets the stated inclusion rule.
