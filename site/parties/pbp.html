TDs Vote Today on People Before Profit Motion for €500 Electricity Credits Funded by 20 Cent Levy on Data Centres

People Before Profit TDs brought a motion to the Dáil on 23 September 2026 calling for a 20 cent per kilowatt-hour levy on data centre electricity consumption to fund €500 household electricity credits, and for a ban on new data centres.

Source metadataAI-assisted screening and summaryNot human-validated
Households in Ireland pay the highest electricity prices in the EU. At about €0.40 per kilowatt hour, that's 38% higher than the EU average. However, data centres, which consume a quarter of our electricity, pay an average of about €0.20 per kWh, half the price paid by households.
Richard Boyd Barrett
This €0.20 levy would raise €1.5 billion , more than enough to pay for a €500 annual electricity credit for every household and an additional €500 electricity credit for households with people with disabilities.
Richard Boyd Barrett

Not one of them would, and yet they expect the public to live with it.
Gerry Carroll
This disaster was created by the Going for Growth strategy the Executive parties and official opposition signed up to, by factory farms waved through on the lough shore, by underinvestment in sewage infrastructure and by a total failure to crack down on agri-polluters.

Budget Office: 95% of Households Are Better Off Under People Before Profit’s Proposals Than Any Others

People Before Profit released Budget 2026 proposals that the Parliamentary Budget Office analyzed as benefiting 95% of households with average income gains of 9.4%, funded by €46.4 billion in net revenue raising primarily through corporation and capital taxes.

Source metadataAI-assisted screening and summaryNot human-validated
The report from the Parliamentary Budget Office demonstrates just how progressive People Before Profit's 2026 budget proposals are. Under our proposals, 95% of households would be better off, while the wealthiest 5% and corporations would pay significantly higher taxes to begin the redistribution of income and wealth that is so urgently required.
Richard Boyd Barrett
By taxing multi-millionaires and big corporations, we demonstrated that we can increase average incomes for middle income households by 14.2%. According to the PBO this compares to 1.4% increase from the Social Democrats' proposals for middle income earners, 3.2% by Labour and 4.5% by Sinn Féin.
Richard Boyd Barrett
