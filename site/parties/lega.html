La Settimana Leghista #37 - Lega per Salvini Premier

Lega announces its annual Pontida rally for September 20, 2026 with the slogan "Non passa lo straniero" (The foreigner shall not pass), while proposing policy measures including deploying 10,000 military personnel for street security, pension reform allowing retirement at 64, flat tax increases, and opposition to EU housing regulations.

NON PASSA LO STRANIERO

Sarà una Pontida di battaglia. Da sempre c'è chi vorrebbe mettere a tacere la Lega e il suo popolo, chi ci ha dati per finiti e chi ha provato a cancellare le nostre idee. Eppure siamo ancora qui, più determinati che mai a difendere ciò in cui crediamo.
It will be a Pontida of battle. There have always been those who would like to silence the Lega and its people, those who have written us off and those who have tried to erase our ideas. Yet we are still here, more determined than ever to defend what we believe in.
