SV vil sette et rødt og grønt stempel på Kommune-Norge

Socialist Left Party working group led by Andreas Sjalg Unneland presents policy document "Green and Fair Local Communities" proposing free kindergarten, expanded municipal housing role, and zero-vision for development of valuable nature ahead of 2027 municipal elections.

Source metadataAI-assisted screening and summaryAI translationNot human-validated
Vi skal kunne sette et tydelig SV-stempel på Kommune-Norge. Det skal bety noe for folk at SV sitter med makten. Vi vil ha kommuner som reduserer forskjeller, bygger ut velferden og tar vare på naturen.
We shall be able to put a clear SV stamp on Municipal Norway. It shall mean something for people that SV holds power. We want municipalities that reduce differences, expand welfare and take care of nature.
Andreas Sjalg Unneland
Vi har gratis skole fordi vi mener alle barn skal ha de samme mulighetene, uavhengig av foreldrenes lommebok. Nå er tiden inne for å ta neste steg og gjøre barnehagen gratis.
We have free school because we believe all children shall have the same opportunities, regardless of their parents' wallet. Now the time has come to take the next step and make kindergarten free.
Andreas Sjalg Unneland

party document Policy proposal

SVs alternative budsjett: – En vanlig jobb skal være nok til å leve gode liv

The Socialist Left Party presented its alternative budget for 2027, proposing major spending increases on housing construction, welfare, climate measures, and tax cuts for lower incomes, funded partly by higher wealth taxes on fortunes over 100 million kroner.

Source metadataAI-assisted screening and summaryAI translationNot human-validated
Folk får det bedre med vårt budsjett
People will be better off with our budget
Marthe Hammer
Vi lever i en tid med voksende ulikhet, en mer urolig verden og en klima- og naturkrise med allerede enorme konsekvenser. De siste årene har hverdagen blitt tøffere for mange av oss. Politikken må vise at det er mulig å gi folk en bedre og tryggere hverdag. Det gjør vi med vårt alternative budsjett.
We live in a time of growing inequality, a more turbulent world and a climate and nature crisis with already enormous consequences. In recent years, everyday life has become tougher for many of us. Politics must show that it is possible to give people a better and safer everyday life. We do that with our alternative budget.
Marthe Hammer

NO2026-10-19#
party document Policy proposal

Tannhelsereformen: Får nye rødgrønne ben å gå på!

The Socialist Left Party (SV) announces that a comprehensive dental health reform proposal is being put out for consultation, building on five years of budget negotiations with Labour and Centre parties that have already given over 550,000 Norwegians cheaper dental care, with the proposal including free necessary orthodontics for children and phased expansion of dental rights starting with younger age groups.

Source metadataAI-assisted screening and summaryAI translationNot human-validated
For SV har det lenge vært klart at tennene er en del av kroppen, da skal ikke ommeboka avgjøre om du får den helsehjelpa du trenger.
For SV it has long been clear that teeth are part of the body, so the wallet should not determine whether you get the healthcare you need.
Kirsti Bergstø
Dette har vi kjempa for siden 2004, og endelig sier flertallet at det ikke lenger skal koste mer å gå til tannlegen enn til legen
We have fought for this since 2004, and finally the majority says it should no longer cost more to go to the dentist than to the doctor
Kirsti Bergstø
