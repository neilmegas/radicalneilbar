LU2026-10-01#
party document Organisational change

déi Lénk

déi Lénk announces that Ana Correia da Veiga and Gary Diderich will replace Marc Baum and David Wagner as MPs according to the party's rotation principle, three years after the legislative elections, and organizes a series of public meetings across Luxembourg in October 2026.

Source metadataAI-assisted screening and summaryAI translationNot human-validated
Geméiss dem Rotatiounsprinzip iwwerhuelen d' Ana Correia da Veiga an de Gary Diderich d'Relève mat därselwechter Energie, awer mat hirer ganz eegener Handschrëft.
In accordance with the rotation principle, Ana Correia da Veiga and Gary Diderich will take over with the same energy, but with their own personal touch.
déi Lénk
De Marc Baum an den David Wagner hunn déi ganz Oppositioun no lénks gedriwwen an Drock op d'Regierung ausgeüübt.
Marc Baum and David Wagner have pulled the entire opposition to the left and exerted pressure on the government.
