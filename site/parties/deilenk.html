party document Organisational change

déi Lénk

déi Lénk announces parliamentary rotation where MPs Ana Correia da Veiga and Gary Diderich will replace Marc Baum and David Wagner three years after legislative elections, with a series of public meetings scheduled across Luxembourg in October 2026.

Geméiss dem Rotatiounsprinzip iwwerhuelen d'Ana Correia da Veiga an de Gary Diderich d'Relève mat därselwechter Energie, awer mat hirer ganz eigener Handschrëft.
According to the rotation principle, Ana Correia da Veiga and Gary Diderich will take over with the same energy, but with their own personal touch.
déi Lénk
De Marc Baum an den David Wagner hunn déi ganz Oppositioun no lénks gedriwwen an Drock op d'Regierung ausgeüübt.
Marc Baum and David Wagner have pulled the entire opposition to the left and exerted pressure on the government.
