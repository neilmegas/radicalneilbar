For years we Freedom Party members have warned about the COFAG self-service shop, an opaque monster created by the black-green government to squander billions in tax money without parliamentary control under the guise of pandemic control.
Dagmar Belakowitsch
Unter einer FPÖ-geführten Bundesregierung mit einem Volkskanzler Herbert Kickl werden die Selbstbedienungsläden für Systemgünstlinge ein für alle Mal zugesperrt. Wir werden diesen Sumpf trockenlegen und sicherstellen, dass jeder Euro dem Wohl der österreichischen Bevölkerung dient und nicht in den Taschen von dubiosen Netzwerken verschwindet!
Under an FPÖ-led federal government with a people's chancellor Herbert Kickl, the self-service shops for system favorites will be closed once and for all. We will drain this swamp and ensure that every euro serves the welfare of the Austrian population and does not disappear into the pockets of dubious networks!
Dagmar Belakowitsch

FPÖ leader Herbert Kickl criticized EU Commission President von der Leyen's State of the Union address for lacking diplomatic peace efforts and focusing on military escalation, calling for Austria to strengthen its neutrality and act as a peace mediator instead of following Brussels' policies.

Frau von der Leyen führt die Europäische Union sehenden Auges weiter auf den Pfaden der Kriegstreiberei. Wer in dieser Rede auch nur ein einziges Wort der Deeskalation oder ernsthafter Friedensbemühungen gesucht hat, wurde bitter enttäuscht.
Mrs. von der Leyen is leading the European Union with open eyes further down the path of warmongering. Anyone who looked for even a single word of de-escalation or serious peace efforts in this speech was bitterly disappointed.
Herbert Kickl
Für uns bedeutet diese Entwicklung im Umkehrschluss, dass wir uns umso stärker auf die immerwährende Neutralität besinnen und die eigene Souveränität vehement einfordern müssen. For us, this development means conversely that we must focus all the more strongly on perpetual neutrality and vehemently demand our own sovereignty. When calls for military armament in Brussels become louder, the Republic must act as an active peace mediator!

Energie-Schmäh der Verlierer-Ampel: Krisenmechanismus als zahnloser Papiertiger

FPÖ energy spokesperson Paul Hammerl criticized the ÖVP-SPÖ-NEOS government's energy crisis mechanism as ineffective due to high trigger thresholds and demanded abolition of national CO2 taxes and EU emissions trading.

People are struggling with high energy costs, and the black-red-pink government presents a crisis mechanism whose hurdles are designed in such a way that it possibly won't take effect this winter.
Paul Hammerl

The national CO2 tax on heating and fuels must be abolished. At the same time, ÖVP Minister Hattmannsdorfer must demand the abolition of CO2 pricing through emissions trading at the EU level.

Christian Hafenecker

In der Verbots-Dauerschleife der Volkspartei

The FPÖ criticizes the ÖVP for rejecting FPÖ proposals to ban political Islam since 2020 while now proposing similar measures, and accuses the ÖVP of enabling mass Muslim immigration and funding deradicalization programs allegedly linked to the Muslim Brotherhood.

Und wer trägt die Verantwortung dafür, dass wir ein solches Verbotsgesetz überhaupt brauchen, weil der radikale Islamismus die Sicherheit der Menschen in unserem Land bedroht? In beiden Fällen lautet die Antwort: Die ÖVP!
To expose the incredibility of Stocker's sudden demand, one only needs to ask two questions: Who bears responsibility for the fact that there is still no ban law against political Islam today? And who bears responsibility for the fact that we need such a ban law at all, because radical Islamism threatens the security of people in our country? In both cases the answer is: The ÖVP!
Herbert Kickl

This raises the suspicion that public money may have been used to promote exactly the development that was supposed to be prevented. This must be clarified down to the last cent and to the last decision
