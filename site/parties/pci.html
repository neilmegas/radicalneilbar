Il PCI al centenario di Akel (Partito Progressista del Popolo Lavoratore) a Cipro

The Italian Communist Party participated as a delegation in the centenary celebration of AKEL (Progressive Party of Working People) in Nicosia, Cyprus, on November 6-7, alongside more than thirty communist parties from around the world, and attended an international seminar on the contemporary left.

The Italian Communist Party issued a statement commemorating the 109th anniversary of the October Revolution, reaffirming its communist identity and calling for continued struggle for socialism against capitalism, imperialism, and inequality.

With the October Revolution the words Socialism and Communism left the field of ideas alone to enter the concreteness of historical realization.

Oggi, in un mondo attraversato da guerre, imperialismo, precarietà e ingiustizie, ricordare la Rivoluzione d'Ottobre non significa guardare nostalgicamente al passato. Significa riconoscere che la necessità di trasformare radicalmente la società è ancora tutta davanti a noi.
Today, in a world crossed by wars, imperialism, precarity and injustices, remembering the October Revolution does not mean looking nostalgically at the past. It means recognizing that the necessity to radically transform society is still entirely before us.

The Ligurian Federation of the Italian Communist Party condemned the detention of Italian volunteers Giorgia Lombardi and Lorenzo Diofili in the West Bank and called on the Italian government to secure their immediate release through diplomatic intervention.

La strategia dello smantellamento

The Italian Communist Party criticizes the 2027 Budget Law presented by the Meloni government, arguing it dismantles collective guarantees through selective tax measures, pension restrictions, and insufficient funding for public health and education while shifting responsibility for rights from the state to individual economic capacity.

Source metadataAI-assisted screening and summaryAI translationNot human-validated
Prima di entrare nel merito delle misure principali, voglio dire che, a monte, la filosofia che governa anche questa Manovra di bilancio è stata quella di concentrare le risorse, non moltissime, che avevamo a disposizione sulle stesse priorità alle quali ci siamo dedicati nei quattro anni che sono passati: famiglia, natalità, riduzione delle tasse, tutela del potere d'acquisto, incentivi al lavoro, incentivi alle imprese, salute dei cittadini
Before going into the details of the main measures, I want to say that, fundamentally, the philosophy governing this Budget Maneuver has been to concentrate the resources, not very many, that we had available on the same priorities to which we have dedicated ourselves in the four years that have passed: family, birth rate, tax reduction, protection of purchasing power, work incentives, business incentives, citizens' health
Giorgia Meloni
Possiamo già affermare che questa manovra non offre risposte adeguate all'emergenza economica e sociale del Paese. Le misure previste non incidono sulle cause dell'impoverimento, non restituiscono potere d'acquisto ai salari e non rafforzano in modo strutturale i servizi pubblici.
We can already state that this maneuver does not offer adequate responses to the country's economic and social emergency. The planned measures do not address the causes of impoverishment, do not restore purchasing power to wages, and do not structurally strengthen public services.
Italian Communist Party

Mozione PCI: approvata “Diritto di scelta”

The Italian Communist Party's motion on preventive conscientious objection was approved by the Assembly of Municipalities of Circondario Empolese Valdelsa, with the right voting against, following the model of Capaci municipality which allows 17-year-olds to declare conscientious objector status preventively.

Source metadataAI-assisted screening and summaryAI translationNot human-validated
È STATA APPROVATA (LA DESTRA HA VOTATO CONTRO) LA MOZIONE 'DIRITTO DI SCELTA', PRESENTATA DAL GRUPPO PCI DELL'ASSEMBLEA DEI COMUNI CIRCONDARIO EMPOLESE VALDELSA.
THE MOTION 'RIGHT OF CHOICE' WAS APPROVED (THE RIGHT VOTED AGAINST), PRESENTED BY THE PCI GROUP OF THE ASSEMBLY OF MUNICIPALITIES CIRCONDARIO EMPOLESE VALDELSA.
Italian Communist Party
Con una delibera di Giunta, il Comune di Capaci, la delibera n°80 del 6 maggio 2026, ha introdotto il principio della obiezione di coscienza preventiva, invitando tutti i diciassettenni (e i loro genitori, trattandosi di minorenni) ad inviare all'ufficio comunale un'Istanza, con cui comunicano di dichiararsi obiettori di coscienza.
With a council resolution, the Municipality of Capaci, resolution no. 80 of May 6, 2026, introduced the principle of preventive conscientious objection, inviting all seventeen-year-olds (and their parents, being minors) to send to the municipal office a request, with which they communicate their declaration as conscientious objectors.
Susanna Rovai
