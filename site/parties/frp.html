Progress Party leader Sylvi Listhaug criticizes the Labour Party government's budget, claiming tax cuts are offset by increased electricity price surcharges, and proposes FrP's alternative of tax cuts, halving food VAT, and reducing fuel taxes.

Å tro at Arbeiderpartiet kutter skatter og avgifter er som å tro på julenissen
To believe that the Labour Party cuts taxes and fees is like believing in Santa Claus
Sylvi Listhaug
Regjeringen har forsøkt å skape et inntrykk av at de gir folk skattelette, men øker avgifter og Norgespris på strøm som gjør at svært mange sitter igjen med mindre i lommeboka. Frp vil kutte skattene, halvere matmomsen og redusere drivstoffavgiftene for å gi folk mer økonomisk handlefrihet
The government has tried to create an impression that they are giving people tax relief, but increases fees and the Norway price on electricity which means that very many are left with less in their wallet. FrP will cut taxes, halve the food VAT and reduce fuel taxes to give people more economic freedom
Sylvi Listhaug
