PT2026-09-14#
party document Public statement

Partido Comunista Português

PCP member Bernardino Soares delivered a press conference statement on the 47th anniversary of Portugal's National Health Service (SNS) law, criticizing the government's privatization policies and calling for increased investment in public healthcare, better working conditions for health professionals, and reduced healthcare costs for citizens.

Não é possível aceitar que um dos países com mais baixos rendimentos da União Europeia seja em simultâneo um daqueles em que a população mais paga directamente do seu bolso o acesso à saúde, mais de 30% do total da despesa, num valor de 9 mil milhões de euros no último ano.
It is not possible to accept that one of the countries with the lowest incomes in the European Union is simultaneously one of those where the population pays the most directly out of pocket for access to healthcare, more than 30% of total expenditure, amounting to 9 billion euros in the last year.
Bernardino Soares
O Governo não resolveu nenhum problema e acentuou muitos dos que existiam. Tem uma única aposta e prioridade: favorecer os interesses dos grupos privados.
The Government has not solved any problem and has accentuated many of those that existed. It has a single bet and priority: to favor the interests of private groups.
Bernardino Soares

The Portuguese Communist Party denounced the European Central Bank's 25 basis point interest rate increase to 2.5%, announced mobilisation actions including a national protest day on 17 September 2026 against rising living costs, and proposed a debate in the European Parliament on interest rate increases.

o Banco Central Europeu decide provocar um aumento de 25 pb na taxa de juro de referência, elevando-a para 2,5%, com os impactos que essa decisão tem nos custos de crédito para as empresas, para os particulares e para as famílias
the European Central Bank decides to provoke an increase of 25 basis points in the reference interest rate, raising it to 2.5%, with the impacts that this decision has on credit costs for companies, individuals and families
Vasco Cardoso
