Tino Chrupalla

Immigration

AfD deputy federal spokesperson Stephan Brandner calls for securing borders, consistent punishment, and deportation of migrants following New Year's Eve violence in Berlin, criticizing discussions of fireworks bans instead of immigration control measures.

Finally, we must make it clear that people who came to our country as asylum seekers and now demonstrate their complete contempt for our traditions and values and become criminals have no place in Germany!
Stephan Brandner
