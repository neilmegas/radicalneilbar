JA21 boekt resultaten bij de Algemene Politieke Beschouwingen

JA21 secured passage of two motions during the 2026 General Political Debates requiring the government to present a plan to prevent broad judicial interpretation of the ECHR before the asylum budget debate and establishing a national ban on face-covering clothing at demonstrations, while also presenting a legislative initiative to prevent permanent closure of Groningen gas wells.
