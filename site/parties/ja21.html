JA21 boekt resultaten bij de Algemene Politieke Beschouwingen

JA21 secured passage of two motions during the 2026 General Political Debates requiring the government to present a plan to prevent broad judicial interpretation of the ECHR before the asylum budget debate and establishing a national ban on face-covering clothing at demonstrations, while also presenting a legislative initiative to prevent permanent closure of Groningen gas wells.

De inzet van JA21 was helder: dit kabinet moet kiezen. Er is een route naar een meerderheid over links en een route over rechts: de Juiste Route.
JA21's position was clear: this cabinet must choose. There is a route to a majority via the left and a route via the right: the Right Route.
JA21
JA21 biedt de enige serieuze, rechtse route naar: Lagere lasten; Minder immigratie; Veilige straten.
JA21 offers the only serious, right-wing route to: Lower taxes; Less immigration; Safe streets.

Prinsjesdag 2026 | Eerdmans: “Kabinet durft geen keuzes te maken”

JA21 leader Joost Eerdmans criticized the government's 2026 budget presentation for failing to make clear choices, stating the party will submit initiatives to reduce tax burden and asylum inflow.

Kabinet durft geen keuzes te maken
Cabinet doesn't dare to make choices
Joost Eerdmans
De prioriteiten van JA21 zijn helder: werkende Nederlanders moeten meer overhouden, de asielinstroom moet fors omlaag, onze veiligheid moet op orde en de overheid moet minder geld uitgeven.
JA21's priorities are clear: working Dutch people must keep more, asylum inflow must decrease substantially, our security must be in order and the government must spend less money.
