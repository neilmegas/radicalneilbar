Statement on the suspension of Workers’ Party TCD committee members

The Workers' Party of Ireland suspended the Workers' Party TCD committee pending disciplinary proceedings after members publicly burned membership cards, accused the party of being a Russian puppet, and supported NATO's role in Ukraine.

Source metadataAI-assisted screening and summaryNot human-validated
The Workers' Party of Ireland condemns the conduct of members of the Workers' Party TCD committee, including the public burning of branch membership cards, their characterisation of the party as oppressive, their false accusations that it acts as a Russian puppet, and their support for NATO's imperialist role in the war in Ukraine.
Workers' Party
For these reasons, the Workers' Party has suspended the Workers' Party TCD committee pending disciplinary proceedings.
