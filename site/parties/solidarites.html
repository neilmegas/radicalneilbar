Non à l’initiative trompeuse sur «la neutralité»

solidaritéS calls for rejection of the Swiss People's Party (UDC) neutrality initiative in the 27 September vote, arguing it facilitates arms trade, serves corporate interests, increases military spending, and prevents sanctions against regimes committing crimes against humanity including Israel.
