FR2026-10-25#
party document Public statement

Trappes et La Verrière : le racisme tue encore une fois

The NPA-l'Anticapitaliste expressed solidarity with victims of a shooting in Trappes and La Verrière on October 23, where a far-right individual killed one Black man and seriously injured five others, including a 12-year-old girl, in what the party characterizes as a racist attack targeting people perceived as Arab and/or Muslim.

Source metadataAI-assisted screening and summaryAI translationNot human-validated
Ce vendredi 23 octobre, un individu d'extrême droite a tiré au pistolet mitrailleur à Trappes et à La Verrière, tuant un homme noir de 26 ans, Pengo Bossio Wangi, et en blessant grièvement cinq autres personnes, toutes visées parce que supposées arabes et/ou musulmanEs, dont une enfant de 12 ans, criblée de balles, visée parce que portant un foulard.
This Friday, October 23, a far-right individual fired a submachine gun in Trappes and La Verrière, killing a 26-year-old Black man, Pengo Bossio Wangi, and seriously injuring five other people, all targeted because they were assumed to be Arab and/or Muslim, including a 12-year-old child, riddled with bullets, targeted because she was wearing a headscarf.
New Anticapitalist Party – The Anticapitalist
Le NPA-l'Anticapitaliste exprime toute sa solidarité avec les victimes, leurs proches et les habitantEs.
The NPA-l'Anticapitaliste expresses its full solidarity with the victims, their loved ones and the residents.
New Anticapitalist Party – The Anticapitalist
