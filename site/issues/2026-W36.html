Barbara Kolm
Vernünftige und verlässliche wirtschaftspolitische Rahmenbedingungen sind die absolute Grundlage für Investitionen, Wettbewerbsfähigkeit und sichere Arbeitsplätze. Das sollten sich Kanzler Stocker, Ministerin Schumann und die gesamte schwarz-rot-pinke Regierung dringend in ihr politisches Stammbuch schreiben
Reasonable and reliable economic policy framework conditions are the absolute foundation for investments, competitiveness and secure jobs. Chancellor Stocker, Minister Schumann and the entire black-red-pink government should urgently write that in their political record book

The end for traditional German car factories is the result of failed economic and energy policy. The one-sided and premature fixation on electric mobility has led the German key industry into a dead end. At the same time, the economic war against Russia and the Iran war are destroying our supply of affordable energy.
Tino Chrupalla
Deutschland braucht endlich wieder eine technologieoffene, ausgewogene Industriepolitik, die das Rückgrat unserer Wirtschaft schützt.
Germany finally needs a technology-open, balanced industrial policy again that protects the backbone of our economy.
