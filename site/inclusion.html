Party-inclusion dossiers

The roster is a documented research decision, not a claim that every monitored party is equivalent or that its self-description matches the project’s operational family.

Inclusion rules

  1. The organisation is an active political party or electoral formation in a monitored European country or Israel.
  2. It contests, or publicly prepares to contest, national, regional, or European elections.
  3. It belongs to the researcher-defined far-left or far-right comparison families used by RAPPORT.
  4. A discoverable official channel is preferred; where none is verified, named-party web and news search is used and disclosed.

Exclusion rules

  1. Historical organisations with no current electoral or organisational activity.
  2. Movements that do not operate as a party or electoral formation.
  3. Routine navigation, event calendars, duplicate syndication, and incidental mentions without a substantive party act.
  4. Records whose date, party attribution, or source cannot be established with sufficient confidence.
