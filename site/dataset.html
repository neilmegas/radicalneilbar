Use and limitations

These are records retained by an automated collection and screening system, not a complete census of political activity. Dynamic, blocked, deleted, or unconfigured sources may be missed. Verify analytical claims against the linked primary source and cite the specific weekly issue and record. See the full methodology and suggested citation.

Stable links

Weekly reports use issues/YYYY-Www.html; individual records add #item-ID; party pages use parties/PARTY-ID.html. These identifiers do not change when the site is rebuilt.
