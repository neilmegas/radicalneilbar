Unit of analysis

One dated, source-addressable party document, parliamentary intervention, leader statement, or external report describing a substantive observable act by or about one monitored party. A record is not a claim, sentence, social-media post fragment, party-week, or ideological theme.

Use and limitations

These are records retained by an automated collection and screening system, not a complete census of political activity. Dynamic, blocked, deleted, or unconfigured sources may be missed. Verify analytical claims against the linked primary source and cite the specific weekly issue and record. See the full methodology and suggested citation. The teaching sample is not independently human-validated unless a row explicitly says researcher-coded.

Stable links

Weekly reports use issues/YYYY-Www.html; individual records add #item-ID; party pages use parties/PARTY-ID.html. These identifiers do not change when the site is rebuilt.
