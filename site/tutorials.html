Usage tutorials

Short workflows for reading RAPPORT as evidence rather than as an authoritative summary of everything a party did.

Read one weekly report

Begin with the Research Passport. Check how many parties had an archived collection attempt and how many records were screened and retained.
Read The week in one minute. Follow each point to its stable evidence record instead of citing the overview alone.
Open Collection coverage. Distinguish a checked zero from an inaccessible source or a missing run.
Inspect provenance. Use the original URL, captured date, source class, model and prompt version, translation status, and human-review label.

Compare parties or periods

Open Compare, choose a date range and party, country, evidence, or document-type filters.
Treat chart counts as retained-document counts, not public support, ideological intensity, or total political activity.
Download the figure and the exact filtered CSV or JSON used to make it.

Reuse data in research or teaching

Download annual corpus shards or weekly CSV files from the dataset page.
Start with the replication notebook and preserve the dataset version, download date, filters, and stable record links.
Use the teaching sample only for exercises. Its AI summaries are not independently human-validated unless the row explicitly says otherwise.
