“Our second best ever by-election result” – Zack Polanski reacts to Holborn and St Pancras by-election

Green Party Leader Zack Polanski achieved the party's second-best parliamentary by-election result ever in Holborn and St Pancras, receiving 33% of the vote and more than tripling the 2024 vote share, while Labour's Sagal Abdi-Wali won the seat vacated by former PM Keir Starmer.
