Zohran Mamdani Mayoral Campaign Organiser to address Derry Public Meeting

People Before Profit is hosting a public meeting in Derry on 21 October featuring Emma Bally, a coordinator from Zohran Mamdani's New York mayoral campaign, to discuss lessons from the socialist candidate's election victory.

Source metadataAI-assisted screening and summaryNot human-validated
We're looking forward to hearing from Emma and the work of Democratic Socialist of American members in the Zohran Mamdani campaign. Mamdani's mayoral victory has created hope across the United States and internationally that change is possible. In a highly contested election, New Yorkers choose the pro-Palestine, pro-migrant, pro-right to choose, socialist candidate championing working-class demands, opposition to fascism and war. There are lessons for socialists, trade union activists and progressives in Ireland from the Mamdani campaign and the explosive growth of the socialist movement in the US. Mamdani is Mayor of the city that hosts Wall Street and many of the wealthiest people and corporations in the world. They will not want Mamdani to succeed or for a socialist progressive challenge to the Republican and Democratic Party to emerge.
Shaun Harkin
