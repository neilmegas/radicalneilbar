„Vollenden wir, was Jörg Haider begonnen hat!“

The FPÖ held a commemorative event in Innsbruck on September 13, 2026, marking the 40th anniversary of Jörg Haider's election as party leader in 1986, with Herbert Kickl calling to complete Haider's political project and presenting Haider's widow with a new party medal.
