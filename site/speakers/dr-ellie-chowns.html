Dr Ellie Chowns urges PM to publish a roadmap for phasing out fossil fuel company tax breaks at COP31

Green Party Parliamentary leader Dr Ellie Chowns wrote to the Prime Minister urging publication of a roadmap to phase out fossil fuel company tax breaks at COP31, strengthen the Oil and Gas Revenue Levy, and push for a global profit tax on oil companies through the UN Tax Convention.

Source metadataAI-assisted screening and summaryNot human-validated
Less than ten years ago the UK took pride in being a climate leader on the global stage, with overwhelming cross-party consensus on the need for urgent action to reduce emissions and transition away from climate-wrecking fossil fuels. How quickly we have fallen.
Dr Ellie Chowns
Against the political backdrop of the Tories abandoning their commitment to net zero and Reform UK adopting outright climate-denialism, it is unforgiveable that Andy Burnham's Labour is also now taking shocking steps backward when it comes to our climate commitments: approving Jackdaw, refusing to rule out greenlighting Rosebank oilfield, and cutting funding for vital home insultation and clean heating programmes.
