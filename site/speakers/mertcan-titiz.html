Stoppt die Repression in der Türkei

The Swiss Party of Labour condemns repression against socialists, LGBTQ+ community, and activists in Turkey following police raids, and expresses solidarity with its sister party SYKP and its co-chair Mertcan Titiz who was arrested during a house search and later released.
