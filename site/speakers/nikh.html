ΝΙΚΗ για ανάρτηση Μητσοτάκη: Φτάνει με τις φτιασιδωμένες κουτοπονηριές

NIKH criticized Prime Minister Mitsotakis's economic policy, particularly his proposal to seek EU permission to return part of increased VAT revenues as support rather than reducing VAT or excise taxes, and proposed permanent reductions in excise tax and VAT instead of temporary subsidies.
