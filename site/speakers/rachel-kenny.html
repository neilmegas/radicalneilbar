People Power Stops Orange Order at Garvaghy - PBP Representatives Join Overnight Blockade

People Before Profit representatives including MLA Gerry Carroll, TD Paul Murphy, and Dublin City Councillor Conor Reddy joined residents in an overnight blockade of Garvaghy Road to prevent an Orange Order march, with over a thousand people participating in solidarity.
