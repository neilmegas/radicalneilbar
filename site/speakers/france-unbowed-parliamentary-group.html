Alerte : l’antifasciste Gino ne doit pas être livré à l’Allemagne

France Unbowed's parliamentary group demands the French government oppose the extradition of antifascist activist Gino to Germany, warning he risks subsequent transfer to Hungary where activists have been imprisoned, and pledges citizen protection initiatives.
