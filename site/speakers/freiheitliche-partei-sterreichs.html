Immigration

The FPÖ criticizes the SPÖ social minister's new social welfare plans as creating incentives for migration into Austria's social system and calls for tighter restrictions on benefit recipients.

SPÖ-Pläne schaffen massive Anreize für Migration in unser Sozialsystem, anstatt Daumen für Leistungsverweigerer enger zu schrauben.
SPÖ plans create massive incentives for migration into our social system, instead of tightening the screws on those who refuse to work.
