The Wealth Tax: Creating a Fairer, Greener Economy

The Green Party proposes a wealth tax of 1-2% on assets over £10 million, alongside capital gains tax reform and alignment of investment income tax rates, claiming these measures would generate over £30 billion annually to fund insulation programmes, public water control, and free school meals.

The Green Party's proposal is highly targeted. It wouldn't affect the homes, pensions, or savings of over 99.9% of the population. Instead, it applies only to those with assets worth more than £10 million. For the 0.04% at this level, a contribution of just 1% (or 2% for billionaires) would be a marginal amount, but it would be life-changing for our country.
Marcus Thornley
The tax on assets and other elements of Green Party tax proposals generate over £30 billion a year: Reforming Capital Gains Tax to align the rates paid by taxpayers on income and taxable gains Aligning tax rates on investment income with the tax and National Insurance Contribution rates on employment income
