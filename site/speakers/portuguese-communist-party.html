The Portuguese Communist Party announced it will present parliamentary initiatives to ensure state funding for 20-euro intermodal passes in metropolitan areas from January 1st, extend 20-euro passes to all intermunicipal communities covering all transport modes, provide free passes for those over 65, and invest in expanding public transport services including railway maintenance.
