GR2026-09-07#
NIKINIKIparty document
Israel and Palestine

NIKI party criticizes the Greek government's procurement of the Israeli "Shield of Achilles" air defense system, questioning whether Greece will have full operational control or remain dependent on Israel for access to source code and system operation.

Ο κ. Πατέλ δήλωσε ξεκάθαρα ότι η Ελλάδα δεν θα έχει πρόσβαση στον πηγαίο κώδικα του συστήματος Command & Control, καθώς αυτός δεν περιλαμβάνεται στην άδεια χρήσης, προσθέτοντας ότι το Ισραήλ θα βρίσκεται δίπλα μας για υποστήριξη και συντήρηση.
Mr. Patel stated clearly that Greece will not have access to the source code of the Command & Control system, as this is not included in the license, adding that Israel will be beside us for support and maintenance.
NIKI
Η ΝΙΚΗ ξεκαθαρίζει ότι στην άμυνα της πατρίδας δεν χωρούν εξαρτήσεις, αστερίσκοι και «άδειες χρήσης» που αφήνουν κρίσιμες δυνατότητες στα χέρια τρίτων.
NIKI clarifies that in the defense of the homeland there is no room for dependencies, asterisks and 'licenses' that leave critical capabilities in the hands of third parties.
NIKI

Dimitris Natsios denounced from the floor of Parliament that the new elementary school books introduce the presentation of same-sex couples with children as a form of family.

NIKI demands the immediate resignation of Sofia Zacharaki and the withdrawal of the controversial books before the first bell rings in one week.
