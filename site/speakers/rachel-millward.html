Lobbyists should not be in the driving seat when it comes to data centres and AI

Green Party Deputy Leader Rachel Millward criticized the Labour government for allowing AI industry lobbyists disproportionate access while excluding environmental and community groups from data centre policy decisions, calling for a halt to new data centre approvals due to water and carbon concerns.

Source metadataAI-assisted screening and summaryNot human-validated
Labour is diving headfirst into AI without a roadmap, charmed by corporate lobbyists while community and green voices are shut out. We need to slam the brakes on new data centres before they drain our water supplies and wreck the UK's carbon budget.
Rachel Millward
When policy is written by corporations, decisions benefit shareholder profits rather than the public good. It's a dangerous lack of oversight, proven by a recent FOI showing the government doesn't even know what current data centres are being used for.
Rachel Millward
