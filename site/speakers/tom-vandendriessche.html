BE2023-01-18#
Vlaams BelangVlaams Belangpress reporting speech

Vlaams Belang criticized the European Parliament's distribution of vice-presidencies after their ID faction candidate received 185 votes but was excluded from the position due to agreements between traditional parties, calling the EU undemocratic and corrupt.

Op basis van haar getalsterkte en zetelaantal zou het logisch zijn dat ook de ID-fractie een ondervoorzitter zou kunnen leveren.
Based on its numerical strength and number of seats, it would be logical that the ID faction could also provide a vice-president.
Gerolf Annemans
Deze Europese Unie is ondemocratisch en door en door corrupt.
This European Union is undemocratic and thoroughly corrupt.
Tom Vandendriessche

BE2023-01-14#
Vlaams BelangVlaams Belangpress reporting speech
Immigration

Vlaams Belang MEP Tom Vandendriessche responds to Frontex data showing 330,000 illegal border crossings in 2022 by calling for reception in migrants' own regions, loss of asylum rights for illegal entrants, and rejection of the EU Migration Pact.

Wie illegaal naar Europa komt, moet elk recht op asiel verliezen
Whoever comes to Europe illegally must lose every right to asylum
Tom Vandendriessche
Europa is voor gelukszoekers het beloofde land. Door de jarenlange verwaarlozing van een streng asielbeleid, blijft de illegale instroom maar toenemen
Europe is the promised land for fortune seekers. Due to years of neglect of a strict asylum policy, the illegal influx continues to increase
Tom Vandendriessche
