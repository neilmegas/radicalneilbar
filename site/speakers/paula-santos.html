PCPPortuguese Communist Partyparty document Public statement

Partido Comunista Português

Paula Santos, President of the Parliamentary Group and member of the Political Committee of the Central Committee, criticized the PSD/CDS government for lacking effective solutions to address the rising cost of living and proposed measures including diplomatic intervention to stop war and sanctions, wage increases, a 50-euro pension increase effective from July 1, price controls on essential goods and services, and support for affected sectors.

O Primeiro-Ministro perdeu uma oportunidade para apresentar soluções efectivas para enfrentar o aumento do custo de vida, optando por mais uma acção de propaganda, com o anúncio de medidas que, em grande parte, já tinham sido apresentadas.
The Prime Minister missed an opportunity to present effective solutions to face the increase in the cost of living, opting for yet another propaganda action, with the announcement of measures that, for the most part, had already been presented.
Paula Santos
Ficou claro que o Governo PSD/CDS, a par de IL, Chega e PS, se recusa a beliscar um euro dos lucros dos grandes grupos económicos, obtidos à custa dos sacrifícios impostos aos salários e às pensões.
It became clear that the PSD/CDS Government, alongside IL, Chega and PS, refuses to touch a euro of the profits of the large economic groups, obtained at the cost of sacrifices imposed on wages and pensions.
Paula Santos
