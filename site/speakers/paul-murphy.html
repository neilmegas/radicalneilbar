Online Public Meeting: Make the Data Centres Pay

People Before Profit is holding a public meeting on September 21st, 2026 to mobilise support for a Dáil motion scheduled for September 23rd that would impose a charge on data centres to fund a €500 energy credit for every household, arguing that data centres currently receive half-price electricity compared to households.

The Data Centres operating in Ireland can easily pay for normal Irish energy prices, but THEY get it HALF PRICE. Their prices should go up, and ours should come down through our plan for a 500 euro energy credit.
People Before Profit
Currently Data Centres get half-price electricity compare to households, the PBP motion would close that gap with a charge on data centres which would fund a €500 energy credit for every household.
People Before Profit

Budget Office: 95% of Households Are Better Off Under People Before Profit’s Proposals Than Any Others

People Before Profit released Budget 2026 proposals that the Parliamentary Budget Office analyzed as benefiting 95% of households with average income gains of 9.4%, funded by €46.4 billion in net revenue raising primarily through corporation and capital taxes.

The report from the Parliamentary Budget Office demonstrates just how progressive People Before Profit's 2026 budget proposals are. Under our proposals, 95% of households would be better off, while the wealthiest 5% and corporations would pay significantly higher taxes to begin the redistribution of income and wealth that is so urgently required.
Richard Boyd Barrett
By taxing multi-millionaires and big corporations, we demonstrated that we can increase average incomes for middle income households by 14.2%. According to the PBO this compares to 1.4% increase from the Social Democrats' proposals for middle income earners, 3.2% by Labour and 4.5% by Sinn Féin.
Richard Boyd Barrett

People Before Profit-Solidarity Think-In: €500 electricity credit funded by data centres levy

People Before Profit-Solidarity held a Think-In on 10 September 2026 proposing a €500 electricity credit funded by a 20c per kWh levy on data centres, wealth taxes on multi-millionaires, price caps on energy and groceries, and announced plans to use Private Members Business time in the Dáil to demand data centres pay household electricity rates.

At our Think-In we will discuss the cost-of-living-crisis and our proposals for meaningful measures to address it. One of our key proposals is for €500 electricity credits for households to be funded by an electricity consumption levy of 20c per kwh on data centres. This would make a big difference to households facing crippling energy bills this winter.
Paul Murphy
We will use our Private Members Business time in the Dáil next week to demand that data centres pay the same for their electricity as householders and that the funds raised be used to fund €500 electricity credits for households.
Paul Murphy
