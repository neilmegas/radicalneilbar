People Before Profit Want to Ban Data Centres - You Do Too!

People Before Profit calls for a comprehensive ban on new data centres, citing that they consume over 23% of Ireland's electricity while paying half the residential rate, and proposes a €1.5 billion annual levy to fund household energy credits, building on parliamentary bills introduced in 2021 and 2023.
