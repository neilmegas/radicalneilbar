O'Neill: Girls Themselves Won U-Turn on Right to Wear Trousers Through Relentless Efforts

People Before Profit representative Maeve O'Neill praised girls who campaigned for the right to wear trousers in school, criticized Education Minister Paul Givan for his U-turn, and called for further reforms including universal free school meals, improved RSE, and health awareness training.

We are delighted girls have won the right to wear trousers in school, if they choose. Education Minister Paul Givan deserves absolutely no credit for his embarrassing U-turn.
Maeve O'Neill
Many girls were subject to discipline and left in fear for campaigning to wear trousers. Their bravery should be fully recognised.
Maeve O'Neill
