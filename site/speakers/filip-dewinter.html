BE2023-01-11#
Vlaams BelangVlaams Belangpress reporting speech

Vlaams Belang launches a petition for military deployment against drug violence in Antwerp under 'Operation Hades', criticizing N-VA for only supporting the measure after an eleven-year-old girl was killed in a drug-related shooting.

Het is jammer dat er eerst een onschuldig kind moest sterven, vooraleer de partij van Bart De Wever ook tot dit inzicht komt
It is unfortunate that an innocent child first had to die before the party of Bart De Wever also comes to this insight
Filip Dewinter
Wij pleiten hier met Operatie Hades al geruime tijd voor en hebben hiervoor een petitie gestart. Laten we hopen dat het momentum er nu wel is om de strijd tegen de drugsmaffia op te voeren.
We have been advocating for this with Operation Hades for quite some time and have started a petition for it. Let us hope that the momentum is now there to step up the fight against the drug mafia.
Filip Dewinter

BE2023-01-09#
Vlaams BelangVlaams Belangpress reporting speech

Vlaams Belang called for an early convening of the Antwerp city council after an eleven-year-old girl died in a shooting linked to drug violence in Merksem, demanding intensified action against drug crime including military assistance.

Het was helaas niet de vraag of, maar wanneer het drugsgeweld in Antwerpen dodelijke slachtoffers zou maken
Unfortunately it was not a question of if, but when the drug violence in Antwerp would claim deadly victims
Filip Dewinter
Hopelijk doet dit spijtig dieptepunt iedereen, over de partijgrenzen heen, beseffen dat we een versnelling hoger moeten schakelen in de strijd tegen de drugsmaffia.
Hopefully this regrettable low point makes everyone, across party lines, realize that we must shift into higher gear in the fight against the drug mafia.
Filip Dewinter
