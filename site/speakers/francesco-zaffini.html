Pubblica amministrazione: decreto rafforza efficienza e qualità dei servizi

Brothers of Italy senators announce final approval of a public administration decree that extends municipal reporting deadlines, creates a 187.5 million euro fund for disability and poverty support, reserves up to 20% of public sector positions for flexible contract workers, restores vacation pay indemnities, and authorizes 500 additional State Police hires.
