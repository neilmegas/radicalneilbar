La France insoumise issued a statement on the opening of the trial for the 2022 murder of Argentine rugby player Federico Martin Aramburu by far-right activists Loïk Le Priol and Romain Bouvier, stating that the far-right is responsible for 90% of ideological murders and pledging to oppose it in parliament, elections, and the streets.

Les deux militants d'extrême droite ont, selon la vidéosurveillance et les multiples témoins, pourchassé Federico Martin Aramburu et Shaun Hegarty, l'un de ses amis rugbyman, après une bagarre entre les 4 hommes dans un bar parisien.
The two far-right activists, according to video surveillance and multiple witnesses, chased Federico Martin Aramburu and Shaun Hegarty, one of his rugby player friends, after a fight between the 4 men in a Parisian bar.
La France insoumise
Cet assassinat commis par des néonazis nous met face à la violence meurtrière de l'extrême droite, qui est responsable de 90% des meurtres idéologiques.
This assassination committed by neo-Nazis confronts us with the murderous violence of the far-right, which is responsible for 90% of ideological murders.
La France insoumise

For several hours, serious accusations about her possible possession of drugs during police custody were broadcast on this channel without any caution. This information proved false but nothing was done by the channel to correct this obvious disinformation.

We can only salute the courage of Radio France employees, who defend what remains of the honor of public broadcasting service.

Alors que l'extrême droite progresse partout dans le pays, et que la propagation de son idéologie est accélérée par des médias privés détenus par des patrons réactionnaires, l'audiovisuel public a une responsabilité particulière en matière de lutte contre la diffusion de fausses informations et d'apaisement du débat public.
While the far right is advancing everywhere in the country, and the spread of its ideology is accelerated by private media owned by reactionary bosses, public broadcasting has a particular responsibility in fighting the spread of false information and calming public debate.
La France insoumise

FR2026-09-04#
LFILa France insoumiseparty document

La France insoumise criticizes the Genevard agricultural plan as misdirected, claiming it primarily benefits large agribusiness operations already insured rather than smaller farmers most affected by climate crisis, and fails to address structural issues like price controls or ecological transition.

Le monde agricole a traversé cet été une crise inédite, subissant en première ligne les conséquences du changement climatique et de l'inaction du Gouvernement.
The agricultural sector experienced an unprecedented crisis this summer, suffering on the front lines from the consequences of climate change and government inaction.
La France insoumise
La moitié de l'enveloppe ira à des agriculteurs qui sont déjà assurés, c'est-à-dire une minorité. Ceux dont les pertes ne sont pas assurées auprès d'assureurs privés auront beaucoup moins.
Half of the funding will go to farmers who are already insured, meaning a minority. Those whose losses are not insured with private insurers will receive much less.
La France insoumise

FR2023-01-30#
LFILa France insoumisepress reporting speech

La France insoumise published its parliamentary group's alternative pension reform proposal, advocating for retirement at 60 years with 40 years of contributions, a 32-hour work week, and six weeks of paid vacation, opposing the government's plan to raise the retirement age to 64.

Le modèle de société que nous lui opposons tient en une formule : la civilisation du temps libéré.
The model of society that we oppose to it can be summed up in one formula: the civilization of liberated time.
La France insoumise
Cela signifie, concrètement, de diminuer le temps de travail dans la semaine, dans l'année et dans la vie, grâce à l'application stricte des 35 heures pour aller vers les 32 heures, la sixième semaine de congés payés et la retraite à 60 ans avec 40 annuités.
This means, concretely, reducing working time in the week, in the year and in life, through strict application of the 35-hour week to move toward 32 hours, the sixth week of paid vacation and retirement at 60 years with 40 years of contributions.
La France insoumise

FR2023-01-05#
LFILa France insoumisepress reporting speech

La France insoumise announced its coordination structure on January 3, 2023, with Manuel Bompard designated as coordinator, and declared total opposition to the government's pension reform with plans for mobilization including a march on January 21.

Alors que la première ministre Élisabeth Borne présentera le projet de loi du gouvernement sur les retraites le 10 janvier, nous rappelons notre opposition totale à cette réforme et notre volonté d'une mobilisation la plus large possible pour en obtenir le retrait.
While Prime Minister Élisabeth Borne will present the government's pension reform bill on January 10, we recall our total opposition to this reform and our desire for the broadest possible mobilization to obtain its withdrawal.
La France insoumise
A l'unanimité, Manuel Bompard en a été désigné coordinateur, dans la continuité du travail engagé depuis plusieurs mois, et en lien étroit avec Mathilde Panot, présidente de groupe à l'Assemblée Nationale et Manon Aubry, présidente de groupe au Parlement européen.
Unanimously, Manuel Bompard was designated as coordinator, in continuity with the work undertaken for several months, and in close connection with Mathilde Panot, group president in the National Assembly and Manon Aubry, group president in the European Parliament.
La France insoumise
