BE2023-01-20#
Vlaams BelangVlaams Belangpress reporting speech

Vlaams Belang MP Ellen Samyn calls for concrete action to protect 360 million persecuted Christians worldwide and announces she will question Foreign Minister Hadja Lahbib about pressuring Middle Eastern and African governments to stop persecutions.

Hier moet veel meer aandacht aan besteed worden
Much more attention must be paid to this
Ellen Samyn
Deze verschrikkelijke vervolgingen moet en stoppen en regeringen in het Midden-Oosten en Afrika moeten daartoe onder druk gezet worden.
These terrible persecutions must stop and governments in the Middle East and Africa must be pressured to do so.
Ellen Samyn

BE2023-01-13#
Vlaams BelangVlaams Belangpress reporting speech

Vlaams Belang criticizes the federal Vivaldi government for missing a European Commission coronavirus recovery payment of 847 million euros due to an inadequate pension reform, blaming the PS party for blocking necessary reforms.

Dit uitstel dreigt weer maar eens een afstel te worden
This postponement threatens to become yet another cancellation
Ellen Samyn
België heeft nu ook aan de Europese Commissie getoond dat dit land niet werkt. De PS loopt liever Europees geld mis dan dat ze ook maar één hervorming zou uitvoeren.
Belgium has now also shown the European Commission that this country does not work. The PS would rather miss out on European money than carry out even one reform.
Ellen Samyn

BE2023-01-06#
Vlaams BelangVlaams Belangpress reporting speech

Vlaams Belang MP Ellen Samyn criticizes the Belgian government for ignoring persecution of Christians in Islamic countries, particularly Nigeria, and calls for cutting development aid to countries that do not protect religious minorities.

Wij kaarten dit mensenrechtendossier al jaren aan in het parlement, maar de paars-groene excellenties blijven hier doof en blind voor
We have been raising this human rights dossier in parliament for years, but the purple-green excellencies remain deaf and blind to it
Ellen Samyn
Hoewel ze luidkeels toeteren over discriminatie tegen moslims, blijven ze muisstil wanneer christenen omwille van hun geloof worden aangevallen. Schrijnend hypocriet.
Although they loudly trumpet about discrimination against Muslims, they remain silent when Christians are attacked because of their faith. Painfully hypocritical.
Ellen Samyn

Vlaams Belang calls for Belgium to end negotiations on the nuclear accord with Iran and halt the extradition treaty, citing new Iranian measures enforcing mandatory hijab wearing as evidence that the regime has not changed despite protests following Mahsa Amini's death.

Teheran scherpt de repressieve islamitische kledingvoorschriften vandaag nog verder aan
Tehran is further tightening the repressive Islamic dress codes today
Ellen Samyn
We moeten de onderhandelingen over het nucleair akkoord definitief opbergen en het uitleveringsverdrag stopzetten
We must definitively shelve the negotiations on the nuclear accord and stop the extradition treaty
Ellen Samyn
