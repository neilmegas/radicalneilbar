NIKI MP Aspasia Kouroupaki submitted parliamentary question 5130 on 6 May 2025 regarding government responsibility for serious COVID-19 vaccine adverse events, and proposes creating a nationwide specialized assessment network, scientific registry, and administrative compensation procedure for documented serious injuries.

Source metadataAI-assisted screening and summaryAI translationNot human-validated
