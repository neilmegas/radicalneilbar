Zack Polanski selected as Green Party candidate for Holborn and St Pancras byelection: “I love this place… I’ll fight for you”

Zack Polanski was selected as Green Party candidate for the Holborn and St Pancras by-election scheduled for 8 October 2026, with the party positioning itself as the main challenger to Labour in the former seat of Keir Starmer.

Greens might be the underdogs in this election – but we have the momentum and we can win.
Zack Polanski
I love this place. But I also know times are tough here in Holborn and St Pancras.
Zack Polanski
