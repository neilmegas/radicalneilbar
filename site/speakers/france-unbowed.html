Niche 2026 - Rétablir le droit commun de la propriété en Outre-mer

France Unbowed proposes to repeal 2024 legislation that reduced the acquisitive prescription period to 10 years in overseas territories, arguing it exposes modest families to land dispossession by speculators and demanding a return to common law.

Source metadataAI-assisted screening and summaryAI translationNot human-validated
En 2024, sans concertation, la prescription acquisitive a été ramenée à 10 ans en outre-mer (contre 30 ans ailleurs).
In 2024, without consultation, the acquisitive prescription was reduced to 10 years in overseas territories (compared to 30 years elsewhere).
France Unbowed
Faciliter les sorties d'indivision est nécessaire, mais pas au prix de la dépossession des plus fragiles. Nous demandons donc l'abrogation de ces deux mesures et le retour au droit commun.
Facilitating exits from joint ownership is necessary, but not at the cost of dispossessing the most vulnerable. We therefore demand the repeal of these two measures and a return to common law.
France Unbowed
