France Unbowed parliamentary group demands immediate hearing of France Télévision CEO Delphine Ernotte before the National Assembly's cultural affairs committee and requests a CNIL investigation into France Télévision's near-transfer of viewer data to Israeli company Terrific.

After Israeli interference against its candidates in municipal elections, France Unbowed will act with vigilance and determination against all attempts to manipulate democratic debate and urges the government to act to guarantee the integrity of upcoming elections.
