EOP:n ja SKP:n yhteinen kannanotto vaalirauhan puolesta

The Communist Party of Finland (SKP) and EOP issued a joint statement calling for respectful electoral campaigning, announcing they will remove personal attacks and hate speech from their platforms while welcoming political criticism as parliamentary elections approach.
