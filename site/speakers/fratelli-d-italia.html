Rai: riforma rende servizio pubblico moderno e plurale

Brothers of Italy members of the RAI oversight commission welcomed committee approval of RAI reform legislation that restructures governance to give Parliament 4 of 7 board seats, employees 1 seat, and the Ministry of Economy only 2 seats, claiming this reverses the 2015 Renzi law's executive control and aligns with European Media Freedom Act requirements.

Accogliamo con soddisfazione l'approvazione in Commissione della riforma della Rai. Si tratta di un passaggio storico che finalmente sana un vulnus aperto oltre dieci anni fa con la legge Renzi e che il centrosinistra quando era al governo non ha mai voluto riformare.
We welcome with satisfaction the committee approval of the RAI reform. This is a historic step that finally heals a wound opened over ten years ago with the Renzi law and that the center-left when in government never wanted to reform.

With the new rules, it will no longer be the executive deciding the CEO alone, but rather the Board of Directors in its full and exclusive statutory autonomy.
