11 Οκτωβρίου 1958: Ο Χαράλαμπος Φιλιππίδης πεθαίνει από βασανιστήρια των Άγγλων

The National Popular Front commemorated the death of Charalambos Filippidis on 11 October 1958, stating he died from torture by the British at the Paphos Auxiliary Corps Centre during the EOKA struggle.
