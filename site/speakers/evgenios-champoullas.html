Δηλώσεις Ε. Χαμπουλλά μετά τη συνεδρίαση της Επιτροπής Άμυνας

ELAM MP Evgenios Champoullas, chair of the Parliamentary Defence Committee, stated after a committee session that the state has failed to properly plan the location of National Guard training facilities and firing ranges, calling for immediate cooperation between the Defence and Interior Ministries to establish comprehensive zoning and safety regulations.
