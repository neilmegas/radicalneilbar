Alors qu’Annemasse est sous les balles, Genève inaugure le tram 17

The Geneva Citizens' Movement deposited a motion demanding increased police presence at border crossings and tram 17 stops, citing drug-related violence in neighboring French towns including shootings in Annemasse and Gaillard, and criticizing the canton's celebration of the tram extension without security measures.

A deux semaines seulement de la votation, les faits divers transfrontaliers continuent de se succéder et les gros titres font froid dans le dos.
Just two weeks before the vote, cross-border incidents continue to occur one after another and the headlines are chilling.
Geneva Citizens' Movement
Le MCG était le seul parti à alerter la population contre le risque de créer un lien direct, rapide et permanent entre des quartiers à haut risque et Genève.
The MCG was the only party to alert the population to the risk of creating a direct, rapid and permanent link between high-risk neighborhoods and Geneva.

FAQ – Initiative pour la priorité au logement à Genève

The Geneva Citizens' Movement is collecting signatures for a cantonal legislative initiative requiring subsidized housing applicants to have resided in Geneva for 5 consecutive years out of the last 10 (instead of 4 out of 8), with a deadline of December 8, 2026 to gather 4,272 valid signatures.

Source metadataAI-assisted screening and summaryAI translationNot human-validated
En 2022, les Genevois ont soutenu une loi du MCG pour renforcer la priorité aux personnes qui habitent à Genève. Pourtant, le Conseil d'État a ensuite introduit des exceptions et des dérogations qui permettent de contourner cette règle, sans nouvelle décision du peuple ni accord du Parlement.
In 2022, Geneva residents supported an MCG law to strengthen priority for people who live in Geneva. However, the State Council then introduced exceptions and derogations that allow this rule to be circumvented, without a new decision by the people or agreement from Parliament.
Geneva Citizens' Movement
Nous voulons modifier la loi pour que les personnes qui demandent un logement subventionné aient résidé à Genève pendant 5 années consécutives au cours des 10 dernières années, au lieu de 4 années sur les 8 dernières.
We want to modify the law so that people applying for subsidized housing have resided in Geneva for 5 consecutive years out of the last 10 years, instead of 4 years out of the last 8.
Geneva Citizens' Movement

Le Conseil d’Etat n’arrive pas à gérer la situation

The Geneva Citizens' Movement criticizes the Geneva State Council's 2027 budget, which shows a 546 million deficit despite 500 million revenue increase, and demands cantonal preference in employment while opposing cross-border spending and transfers to France.

Source metadataAI-assisted screening and summaryAI translationNot human-validated
Alors que de nombreux jeunes se retrouvent à l'aide sociale – dont 1600 titulaires d'un titre universitaire – l'Etat continue à engager massivement des frontaliers permis G.
While many young people find themselves on social assistance – including 1600 university degree holders – the State continues to massively hire cross-border workers with G permits.
Geneva Citizens' Movement
Il faudrait au contraire une politique affirmée de préférence cantonale, que réclame le MCG sans être entendu.
On the contrary, we need an affirmed policy of cantonal preference, which the MCG demands without being heard.
Geneva Citizens' Movement
