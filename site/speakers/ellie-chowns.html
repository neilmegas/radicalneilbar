“Small tweaks won’t cut it” on energy bills, say Greens

Green Party Westminster Leader Ellie Chowns responded to reports of Chancellor's budget measures on energy bills by calling for scaling up renewable energy, ending energy company profiteering, public ownership of energy, home insulation, decoupling gas and electricity prices, and scrapping North Sea oil and gas drilling plans.

Source metadataAI-assisted screening and summaryNot human-validated
It should not take 'forecasts' to tell the government that families cannot cope with spiralling energy costs; speak to anyone and they will tell you we're all being ripped off.
Ellie Chowns
Small tweaks won't cut it. To bring down bills for good, we need to scale up renewable energy, end rampant profiteering by energy giants and bring energy back into public hands, insulate homes properly, and fully decouple the price of gas from electricity.
Ellie Chowns

Green Party parliamentary leader Ellie Chowns criticised Conservative proposals to scrap environmental regulations, arguing they weaken climate protection following summer wildfires and falsely blame nature protection for blocking housing development.

Source metadataAI-assisted screening and summaryNot human-validated
