Covid: con Arcuri clamorose falle e sperpero denaro pubblico

Brothers of Italy deputy Francesco Filini criticized former Covid commissioner Domenico Arcuri's management during a Covid commission hearing, highlighting successor General Figliuolo's testimony about discontinuing large contracts, intermediaries, and advance payments that characterized Arcuri's tenure, including 1.25 billion euros spent on 800 million masks.
