ΜέΡΑ25 app

MeRA25 met with the Committee of Organizations and Residents of Agia Paraskevi on 14 September 2026 and declared full support for their demands to protect Mount Hymettus from development, transfer Ministry of Agricultural Development land to the municipality as green space, and coordinate inter-municipal resistance against government plans.
