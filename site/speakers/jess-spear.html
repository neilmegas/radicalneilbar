Councillor Takes High Court Legal Challenge as Council Executive Refuse to Implement 15-to-1 Vote to Halt Rent Increases

People Before Profit Councillor Jess Spear has launched a High Court judicial review against South Dublin County Council after its chief executive refused to implement a 15-to-1 council vote to halt rent increases of up to 53% for social housing tenants.

Source metadataAI-assisted screening and summaryNot human-validated
The law is very clear on this. The Chief Executive has to follow council policy, and he has to abide by Section 140 motions. Civil servants, no matter how senior, are not a law unto themselves. Ploughing ahead with a rent hike against the express wishes of the elected members looks like a clear and reckless violation of the Local Government Act.
Jess Spear
What is happening in South Dublin should alarm anyone who cares about local democracy. Councillors voted not once but twice on this, and an unelected official has decided to override them. If a Chief Executive can simply ignore a Section 140 motion, then what local democracy do we have left at all?
Paul Murphy
