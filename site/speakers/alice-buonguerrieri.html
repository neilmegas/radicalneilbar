Covid: censurarono studio su effetti avversi al vaccino

Brothers of Italy parliamentarians Alice Buonguerrieri and Antonella Zedda used testimony in the Covid Commission to criticize alleged censorship of a 2022 study by ISS researcher Loredana Frasca on adverse vaccine effects, particularly myocarditis in young people, and questioned mass vaccination policies during the pandemic.

Source metadataAI-assisted screening and summaryAI translationNot human-validated
Il clima di caccia alle streghe durante la pandemia di Covid fu da regime totalitario. Giornalisti, esponenti politici, esperti di varie materie e persino ricercatori scientifici furono censurati, ridicolizzati o demonizzati per aver espresso opinioni e studi sgraditi al pensiero allora dominante.
The climate of witch-hunting during the Covid pandemic was like a totalitarian regime. Journalists, political figures, experts in various fields and even scientific researchers were censored, ridiculed or demonized for having expressed opinions and studies unwelcome to the then-dominant thinking.
Alice Buonguerrieri
A chi giovava questa vaccinazione di massa? Ne deve rispondere chi era al governo.
Who benefited from this mass vaccination? Those who were in government must answer for it.
Alice Buonguerrieri
