La mauvaise mémoire n’est pas le problème

solidaritéS publishes an analysis arguing that the AfD's electoral success in Germany (43.8% in Saxe-Anhalt, 38.2% in Mecklembourg-Poméranie-Occidentale on 6 September 2026) cannot be explained by voters forgetting Nazi history, but rather by the contemporary far-right's dissociation of fascist ends from genocidal means, and calls for antifascist politics to address material causes of crisis rather than relying solely on historical memory.

Le 6 septembre 2026, le parti d'extrême droite Alternative für Deutschland a obtenu 43,8% des suffrages aux élections régionales en Saxe-Anhalt et 38,2% deux semaines plus tard dans le Mecklembourg-Poméranie-Occidentale.
On 6 September 2026, the far-right party Alternative für Deutschland obtained 43.8% of votes in regional elections in Saxe-Anhalt and 38.2% two weeks later in Mecklembourg-Poméranie-Occidentale.
Antoine Dubiau
Rappeler les crimes de l'extrême droite à travers l'histoire est insuffisant pour lutter contre ses formes contemporaines.
Recalling the crimes of the far-right throughout history is insufficient to fight against its contemporary forms.
