IL 17 NOVEMBRE AL PARLAMENTO EUROPEO DI BRUXELLES LA CONFERENZA STAMPA “ECLIPSE OF JUSTICE IN EUROPE”

Forza Nuova announces a press conference titled 'Eclipse of Justice in Europe' scheduled for 17 November 2026 at the European Parliament in Brussels, focusing on the events of 9 October 2021 in Rome and their judicial and political implications, with speakers including party secretary Roberto Fiore, vice-secretary Luca Castellini, Polish MEP Grzegorz Braun, and defense lawyer Pablo De Luca.
