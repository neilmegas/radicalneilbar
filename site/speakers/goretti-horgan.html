Harkin: Only Crime is Attempting To Stop Gaza Genocide

People Before Profit Councillor Shaun Harkin condemned a Derry court ruling against Palestine solidarity activists Goretti Horgan and Nina Pulis, calling for resistance to criminalisation of Palestine solidarity and announcing support for an October 10th global day of action for Palestine.

Source metadataAI-assisted screening and summaryNot human-validated
We are very disappointed at the judge's ruling in Derry Court House, but not disheartened. Goretti Horgan, Nina Pulis and Palestine solidarity campaigners trying to stop complicity with genocide don't belong in court. Their only crime is attempting to stop Israel's Gaza genocide.
Shaun Harkin
The criminalisation of Palestine solidarity will not be accepted and will be resisted in the courts and on the streets.
Shaun Harkin
