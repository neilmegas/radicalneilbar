M. Béchu doit rendre des comptes face aux alertes d’ONG signalant le blocage des négociations de la COP15 par la France

La France Insoumise deputies criticize Minister Béchu for allegedly blocking stronger biodiversity protections during COP15 negotiations in Montreal, demanding explanations for France's role in weakening the agreement.

Si la France a effectivement bloqué un accord international qui définit l'avenir de l'habitalité de notre planète, c'est non seulement insensé, mais aussi meurtrier.
If France has effectively blocked an international agreement that defines the future of our planet's habitability, it is not only senseless, but also murderous.
La France insoumise
