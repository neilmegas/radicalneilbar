DE2023-01-03#
AfDAlternative für Deutschlandpress reporting speech

AfD deputy federal spokesperson Peter Boehringer criticizes FDP Finance Minister Lindner for withdrawing tax reduction proposals under pressure from SPD and Greens, arguing that tax relief is urgently needed during inflation and that the FDP achieves nothing in the coalition that does not align with Green party wishes.

In Zeiten, in denen die Inflation den Lebensstandard der Menschen auffrisst, sind steuerliche Entlastungen das Gebot der Stunde.
In times when inflation is eating away at people's standard of living, tax relief is the order of the day.
Peter Boehringer
Für viele hoch ideologische, oftmals sogar kontraproduktive und gegen Deutschland gerichteten Projekte scheint Geld vorhanden zu sein, nicht jedoch für die deutschen Steuerzahler.
For many highly ideological, often even counterproductive and anti-German projects, money seems to be available, but not for German taxpayers.
Peter Boehringer
