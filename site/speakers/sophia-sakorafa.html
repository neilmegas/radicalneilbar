ΜέΡΑ25 app

MeRA25 MP Sophia Sakorafa delivered a parliamentary speech criticizing the government's multi-level governance bill and accusing Sports Deputy Minister Avghenakis of partisan interference in sports federation elections, including attempts to prevent her election as president of SEGAS and to remove a communist-affiliated coach from the board.

Η αθλητική κοινωνία έχει και το όραμά της και τη δύναμή της – Και θα καταφέρουμε να νικήσουμε στο τέλος!
The sports community has both its vision and its strength – And we will manage to win in the end!
Sophia Sakorafa
Η λεγόμενη Πολυεπίπεδη Διακυβέρνηση εμπεριέχει την υπονόμευση – αν όχι και αναίρεση – της ίδιας της Δημοκρατίας.
The so-called Multi-level Governance contains the undermining – if not the abolition – of Democracy itself.
Sophia Sakorafa
