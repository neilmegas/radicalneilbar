LFILa France insoumisepress reporting speech

La France insoumise's parliamentary group expressed concern over Turkey's suspension of public funding for the HDP party and called for an end to prosecutions against the party and restoration of its public financing.

Le groupe LFI-NUPES s'inquiète de cette énième attaque grave contre la démocratie par un pays membre du Conseil de l'Europe.
The LFI-NUPES group is concerned about this latest serious attack on democracy by a member country of the Council of Europe.
La France insoumise
Nous apportons notre soutien aux aspirations démocratiques du peuple turc et demandons la cessation immédiate des poursuites à l'encontre du HDP et prônons le retour de son financement public.
We support the democratic aspirations of the Turkish people and call for the immediate cessation of prosecutions against the HDP and advocate for the restoration of its public funding.
La France insoumise
