Η ΦΩΝΗ ΛΟΓΙΚΗΣ τιμά τη μνήμη του Παύλου Μελά

Voice of Reason commemorated the 122nd anniversary of Pavlos Melas's death by laying a wreath at his statue in Kifisia, criticizing SYRIZA's Prespa Agreement and New Democracy's failure to cancel it.
