MLAs Pocket Pay Rises While Workers Wait

People Before Profit MLA Gerry Carroll and Councillor Shaun Harkin condemned proposals for a further pay rise for MLAs months after a 27% increase, contrasting this with delayed pay awards for public sector workers including health workers and teachers.

This is a slap in the face to health workers, civil servants and every public sector worker still waiting on a pay award.
Gerry Carroll
Five months into the financial year, the Executive has no budget and workers are being told there's no money for fair pay or decent public services. Inexplicably, MLAs are now lined up for another rise on top of the £14,000 they pocketed in April.
Gerry Carroll
