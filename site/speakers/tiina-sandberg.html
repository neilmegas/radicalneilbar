Sri Lanka nousee vasemmistopolitiikalla – SKP tapasi JVP:n delegaation

The Communist Party of Finland met with a delegation from Sri Lanka's ruling communist party JVP, led by Transport Minister Bimal Rathnayake, to discuss JVP's electoral success and Finland's upcoming parliamentary elections.
