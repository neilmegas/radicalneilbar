Genitale verminking bij vrouwen moet gerichter worden aangepakt

Vlaams Belang submits resolutions in multiple Belgian parliaments demanding stricter measures against female genital mutilation, including lifetime professional bans for medical personnel who assist, citing 23,395 affected women and girls in Belgium and linking the practice to Islamic traditions.

Deze vorm van verminking en foltering leidt zo goed als nooit tot enige vervolging of bestraffing
This form of mutilation and torture almost never leads to any prosecution or punishment
Nathalie Dewulf
Door de toenemende migratie komt dit ook meer en meer in het Westen, ook in Vlaanderen, voor.
Due to increasing migration, this is also occurring more and more in the West, including in Flanders.
Nathalie Dewulf
