Treibstoffpreise: Italienische Regierung zeigt, wie es geht

FPÖ MEP Roman Haider praised the Italian government's suspension of vehicle taxes and fuel tax reductions, demanding Austria adopt similar measures including abolishing the Normverbrauchsabgabe (registration tax), reducing motor insurance tax, and lowering fuel taxation.
