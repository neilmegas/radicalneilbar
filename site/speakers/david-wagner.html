LU2026-09-26#
déi LénkThe Leftparty document Organisational change

déi Lénk

The Left (déi Lénk) announces that MPs Ana Correia da Veiga and Gary Diderich will replace Marc Baum and David Wagner according to the party's rotation principle, three years after legislative elections.

Geméiss dem Rotatiounsprinzip iwwerhuelen d' Ana Correia da Veiga an de Gary Diderich d'Relève mat därselwechter Energie, awer mat hirer ganz eegener Handschrëft.
According to the rotation principle, Ana Correia da Veiga and Gary Diderich will take over with the same energy, but with their own personal touch.
déi Lénk
De Marc Baum an den David Wagner hunn déi ganz Oppositioun no lénks gedriwwen an Drock op d'Regierung ausgeüübt.
Marc Baum and David Wagner have pulled the entire opposition to the left and exerted pressure on the government.
