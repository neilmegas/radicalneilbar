Partido Comunista Português

The Portuguese Communist Party announced it will present parliamentary initiatives to fix intermodal transport passes at 20 euros in metropolitan areas from January 1st, extend this pricing to all intermunicipal communities, introduce free transport for those over 65, and invest in expanding public transport capacity including railway maintenance.

O PCP defende a progressiva redução do preço dos transportes públicos a caminho da sua gratuitidade, num processo acompanhado pelo aumento da oferta e da intermodalidade.
The PCP defends the progressive reduction of public transport prices towards their gratuity, in a process accompanied by increased supply and intermodality.
Partido Comunista Português
Nesse sentido o PCP irá apresentar na Assembleia da República iniciativas que assegurem o financiamento a partir da Administração Central dos meios necessários para: - fixar nos 20 euros o valor do passe intermodal nas áreas metropolitanas já a partir de 1 de Janeiro
In this sense, the PCP will present initiatives in the Assembly of the Republic that ensure financing from the Central Administration of the necessary means to: - fix the value of the intermodal pass in metropolitan areas at 20 euros starting from January 1st
