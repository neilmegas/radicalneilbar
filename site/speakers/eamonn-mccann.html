Think Left Conference This Weekend in Derry

People Before Profit is hosting its annual Think Left Conference from October 2-5, 2026 in St Columb's Hall, Derry, featuring international speakers, discussions on workers' campaigns, Palestine solidarity, political unionism, Stormont's future, and socialist feminist perspectives on Irish unity.
