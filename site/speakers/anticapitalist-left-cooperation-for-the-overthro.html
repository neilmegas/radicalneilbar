Δύο νεκροί για τα υπερκέρδη της Motor Oil

ANTARSYA issued a statement condemning a fatal explosion at Motor Oil refineries in Agioi Theodoroi, Corinthia on 9 October 2026 that killed two workers and injured four, calling it an employer crime driven by profit maximization and demanding comprehensive labor reforms including reduced working hours, collective bargaining rights, higher minimum wage, and improved workplace safety inspections.
