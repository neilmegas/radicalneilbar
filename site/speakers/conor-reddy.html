Dublin City Council Housing SPC Backs People Before Profit Call to Reverse Council Rent Increases

People Before Profit Councillor Conor Reddy's motion calling for reversal of Dublin City Council rent increases passed the Housing and Social Inclusion Strategic Policy Committee by 11 votes to 8, ahead of preparations for the 2027 City Council Budget.

Today's vote sends a very clear message to the Chief Executive. A majority of members of the Housing SPC do not believe tenants should be made to carry these rent increases, particularly in the middle of a rapidly worsening cost-of-living crisis.
Conor Reddy
People are understandably angry at being asked to pay more rent for homes that in many cases are still in poor condition. Some tenants are being told that improvements are coming, but may have to wait years to see them. You cannot tell somebody living with damp, mould or a freezing home that they should pay more now and perhaps see the benefit somewhere down the line.
Conor Reddy
