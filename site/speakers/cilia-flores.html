Con il compagno Raul e la rivoluzione Cubana

The Italian Communist Party condemns the arrest warrant issued by Florida's attorney general against Raul Castro, expresses solidarity with Cuba and Venezuela, and calls for mobilization in Italy to defend the Cuban Revolution against what it characterizes as US aggression and economic blockade.

The Italian Communist Party reaffirms and emphasizes its profound esteem and admiration for comrade Raul Castro Ruz, a pillar of the struggle against Batista, of the guerrilla warfare that led to the revolutionary triumph of January 1, 1959, of the construction of socialism in Cuba and its defense against imperialism.
