AfD federal spokesman Tino Chrupalla criticizes the closure of four Volkswagen production sites in Germany, blaming failed economic and energy policy, premature focus on electric mobility, and economic conflicts with Russia and Iran.

Das Aus für traditionsreiche deutsche Autowerke ist das Resultat einer verfehlten Wirtschafts- und Energiepolitik. The end for traditional German car factories is the result of failed economic and energy policy. The one-sided and premature fixation on electric mobility has led the German key industry into a dead end. At the same time, the economic war against Russia and the Iran war are destroying our supply of affordable energy.
Tino Chrupalla
Deutschland braucht endlich wieder eine technologieoffene, ausgewogene Industriepolitik, die das Rückgrat unserer Wirtschaft schützt.
Germany finally needs a technology-open, balanced industrial policy again that protects the backbone of our economy.
