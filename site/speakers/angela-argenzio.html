Green Party responds to continuation of power-sharing in Sheffield

The Green Party of England and Wales issued a statement after Sheffield City Council's Labour Group reversed its decision to end power-sharing with the Green Group, criticizing Labour leadership's attempted intervention and linking it to Labour's position on Israel.
