Gli studenti da tutte le piazze d’Italia: soldi per le aule, non per le armi

The Italian Communist Party expressed active support for student demonstrations on October 16, 2026, across Italy demanding increased education funding instead of military spending, opposing the Valditara decree, criticizing government and EU military expenditures, and announcing participation in upcoming strikes on October 30 and November 20.
