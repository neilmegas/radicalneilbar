Schluss mit dem Herumlavieren, Herr Kanzler!

FPÖ General Secretary Michael Schnedlitz criticizes the ÖVP government for failing to implement a ban on political Islam and calls on them to support the FPÖ's legislative proposal, accusing the ÖVP of enabling mass immigration and Islamization over forty years in government.
