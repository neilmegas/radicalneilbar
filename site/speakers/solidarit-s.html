solidaritéS opposes the financing of the 13th AVS pension through a VAT increase, criticizing the USS for abandoning its earlier position to fund it through social contributions, and will campaign for a left-wing 'no' vote in the November 29 referendum.

During the campaign in favor of the 13th AVS pension, the USS defended financing through contributions. The union umbrella organization has now reversed its position before the bourgeoisie, without even trying to create a balance of power.
