Niche 2026 - Défiscaliser les pensions alimentaires perçues

France Unbowed presents a bill to exempt child support payments received by custodial parents from taxation, index pensions to inflation, and strengthen family support allowances, addressing precarity of single-parent families.

Source metadataAI-assisted screening and summaryAI translationNot human-validated
Aujourd'hui, en plus d'être trop faible (200 euros en moyenne quand un enfant coûte 750 euros) la pension est déduite des revenus du parent qui la verse, mais imposée chez le parent qui la reçoit, dans l'écrasante majorité des cas, la mère.
Today, in addition to being too low (200 euros on average when a child costs 750 euros), the pension is deducted from the income of the parent who pays it, but taxed for the parent who receives it, in the overwhelming majority of cases, the mother.
