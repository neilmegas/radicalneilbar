Responding to the sentencing of Russell Brand for rape and sexual assault yesterday and today’s allegations about David Walliams being made public following his failed legal bid, Green MP Hannah Spencer said: “Nobody should be untouchable. “Powerful men who abuse women, and those who facilitate abuse without acting to stop it, should face justice. “Russell Brand has finally been jailed thanks to the bravery of women who spoke out, with nothing to gain except protecting others. “But with allegations about David Walliams coming to light, it’s clear convicting one man doesn’t fix a broken system. “The culture of how we talk to and treat women and girls has to change. “The Government must listen to survivors and experts and give the fight to keep women and girls safe the urgency it deserves.” Editor’s Notes: Hannah Spencer is the Green’s Parliamentary lead on Digital, Culture, Media and Sport.
