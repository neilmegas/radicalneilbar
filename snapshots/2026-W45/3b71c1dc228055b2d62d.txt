The Green Party would like to extend our condolences to the families and friends of the 106 people a week who lost their lives to drugs in England and Wales in 2025. For 14 years in a row, we saw an increase in drug overdose deaths, and while we are thankful for the tiny reduction this year, we recognise that this figure is still far too high. In any other policy area, governments would be calling for an urgent review into what is going wrong. Instead, we have a Prime Minister who has not even appointed a dedicated drugs minister, almost four months into his premiership. Evidence-based drug policy must be implemented if this government is serious about creating happier, healthier, and safer communities. The Green Party calls for action that the government could immediately take to reduce the number of drug misuse deaths. ● Sanction provision of Overdose Prevention Centres. ● Provide Heroin Assisted Treatment to the most marginalised drug users. ● Increase the provision of Naloxone. ● Make the establishment of drug checking services easier. ● Improve access to Harm Reduction services across the prison estate. The latest figures, released by the Office for National Statistics today, show that in 2025, 5519 people died from drug poisoning. Read the Green Party’s drug policy . Call to Action “Our call to action is to contact your MP now, today, and ask them to act on behalf of people who have little or no voice, to follow the evidence, and make life-saving changes to their policy of inaction and avoidance. Please ask your MP to support the opening of OPCs in their constituencies. Let’s hold the government to account and see the number of unnecessary deaths reduce for the first time in over a decade.” Vicki Beere, Policy Officer, Green Party Drug Policy Working Group.
