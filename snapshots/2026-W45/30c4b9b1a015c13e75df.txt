Power is an American crime drama television series created and produced by Courtney A. Kemp in collaboration with Curtis "50 Cent" Jackson. [1] It aired on the Starz network from June 7, 2014 to February 9, 2020. Upon release, Power gained positive reviews and is one of Starz's highest-rated shows and one of cable's most-watched shows.
