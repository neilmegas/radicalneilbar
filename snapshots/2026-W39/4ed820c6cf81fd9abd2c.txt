Online Public Meeting: Make the Data Centres Pay Search Log in Join Log in Join Public Meeting Online Public Meeting: Make the Data Centres Pay The Data Centres operating in Ireland can easily pay for normal Irish energy prices, but THEY get it HALF PRICE. Their prices should go up, and ours should come down through our plan for a 500 euro energy credit. It makes sense, so let's make it happen. Meet Monday 6pm on Zoom. Details below. 19 Sep 2026 Join us online at 6pm on Monday September 21st for an online public meeting on the upcoming Dáil motion to make data centres pay the same for a unit of electricity as ordinary consumers. Currently Data Centres get half-price electricity compare to households, the PBP motion would close that gap with a charge on data centres which would fund a €500 energy credit for every household. The motion will be voted on in the Dáil on September 23rd, and we want to use it as a springboard for building the wider campaign against the expansion of data centres. We’ll be joined by: Paul Murphy TD Dr Patrick Brodie Ann Louise Bresnehan (Save the Moat) Data centres are putting huge demands on our electricity system and infrastructure while households face rising costs and pressure on public services. The question of who pays for this expansion is also a question about what kind of economy we want, and who it is being run for. The campaign against data centres is growing in communities around the country. Come along, hear from people involved in the fight, and discuss how we can build the campaign further.
