Responding to John Healey’s first Budget as chancellor, Treasury Spokesperson and leader of the Parliamentary Green Party, Dr Ellie Chowns MP, said: “You’d have thought that after going to the trouble of ousting the last PM, taking his job, and installing a brand-new Chancellor, Andy Burnham would be keen to ensure Labour displayed a bit more ambition at today’s Budget than it did under his predecessor. “Unfortunately, what Britain actually saw today was a business-as-usual Budget which failed to engage with the real issues facing our country – choosing to tinker round the edges, rather than properly taxing extreme wealth and profits, making essentials genuinely and permanently affordable, or investing in our long-term climate resilience. “There was no discussion of the climate crisis in Healey’s speech today, nor any new investment in renewables and climate adaptation, despite the devastating impact of this summer’s deadly heatwaves and wildfires on our communities, public services, and economy.
