Responding to reports in the Times today that Labour’s budget will include a freeze on the rate for 18-20 year olds, in spite of a Labour manifesto commitment to equalise the rates with the adult rate by 2029, Green Party deputy leader Mothin Ali said: “Workers’ rights are universal or they are nothing. “Anyone doing a day’s work, needs to be paid the same rate for the same job. A young person’s rent and food bill is no less because they are young, so they need to receive the same wage. “The Greens will introduce a £15 minimum wage for all ages and a proper Youth Jobs Guarantee, which pays and trains young people in real jobs, unlike Labour’s retro 1980s YTS version. “Healey and Burnham are now breaking a manifesto pledge to young people that even Starmer managed to keep. Labour have no respect for young people, who appear to just be there for cheap labour for the wealthy and their corporate donors. “Of course big corporations want to pay their workers less, they made the same nonsense arguments when the minimum wage was introduced and it had no impact at all on jobs, it just made life better and fairer for workers. Labour is now looking like turning its back on its own achievement.” Further information Labour’s 2024 manifesto pledge on the equalization of minimum wage rates, says, “We will remove the discriminatory age bands to ensure every adult worker benefits”.
