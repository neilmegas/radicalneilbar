“The Green Party has grown into a mass-membership movement over the past year, and that growth has driven unprecedented electoral success across all corners of England and Wales. “With that growth comes a need to update our structures and processes to ensure our policy is developed by members in as democratic, robust and inclusive a way as possible. “We have established a working group to look at examples from across the world of how progressive parties with mass memberships develop effective policy and put forward proposals for models that could work well for our party. “This will be an iterative process with the first stage of improvements expected to be brought for a vote at Spring Conference and a delivery plan in place for longer term actions.” The post Joint leadership statement on democratic engagement in the Green Party appeared first on Green Party .
