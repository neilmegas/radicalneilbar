Dr Ellie Chowns MP, Green Party Westminster Leader, responding to reports that the Chancellor is planning budget measures to cut energy bills , said: “It should not take ‘forecasts’ to tell the government that families cannot cope with spiralling energy costs; speak to anyone and they will tell you we’re all being ripped off. “Small tweaks won’t cut it.
