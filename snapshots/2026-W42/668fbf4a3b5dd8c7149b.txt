His 2014 tome, Bitcoin: The New International Currency , played a key role in bringing the cryptocurrency into the mainstream in Latin America, a region scarred by persistent inflation and years of government mismanagement that have made alternatives to state-backed money particularly attractive. Ulrich, however, contends that Bitcoin need not supplant the dollar for the project to work, and he argues that its most meaningful contribution may be to erode the government’s monopoly on money rather than to become the planet’s dominant currency.
