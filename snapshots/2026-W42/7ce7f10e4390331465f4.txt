Commenting on Conservative plans to spend £10bn on a British defence system , inspired by Israel’s Iron Dome air defence system, Green Party spokesperson on defence, Ellie Chowns MP, said: “The Conservatives’ announcement of £10bn over 10 years on a “Britannia Shield” is political posturing and picking round numbers out of a hat to make conference headlines. All proposed military expenditure must be subject to proper scrutiny and analysis of cost effectiveness. The MoD has a terrible record on this, with numerous defence projects over budget or failing to deliver value for money. “And once again, the Tories would go after the most vulnerable in society to fund their militarism, cutting billions in benefits, reimposing the two-child benefit cap, and steering funds away from a scheme funding cars for disabled people. “Genuine security is about far more than weapons systems. We need to look at countering cyber-attacks and being prepared for pandemics, which as we know can kill millions of people. Above all we need to address the security risks posed by climate breakdown, loss of nature and ecosystem collapse.
