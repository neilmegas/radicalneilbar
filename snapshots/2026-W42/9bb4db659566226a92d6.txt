Reconquête (French: [ʁəkɔ̃kɛt], REC; English: Reconquest), stylised as Reconquête! (often shortened as R!), is a far-right political party in France founded in late 2021 by Éric Zemmour, who has since served as its leader. [8][9] He was a candidate in the 2022 presidential election, in which he placed fourth with just over 7% of the vote as the best newcomer.
