Responding to Conservative proposals to scrap a tranche of regulations to protect the environment, Dr Ellie Chowns MP, the leader of the Green group in parliament, said: “Following the devastating wildfires of the summer, that were a direct result of climate breakdown, the Tories have chosen this moment to make a bonfire of environmental standards, weaken climate protection, and abolish organisations charged with protecting nature. “The Conservatives are also once again pretending that protecting nature is a blocker to building the homes and infrastructure this country desperately needs. It isn’t. The real barriers are decades of underinvestment in vital community infrastructure and cruel austerity – the very conditions the Tories seem set to reignite. “And scrapping the rules that secure affordable homes, built to high energy-efficiency and environmental standards is a gift to profiteering developers. It would leave millions of people unable to afford a home and facing higher costs and energy bills. “The Green Party knows that environmental protection and economic prosperity are not opposing goals.
