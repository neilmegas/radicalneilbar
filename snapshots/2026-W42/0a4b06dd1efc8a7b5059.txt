The IFS has warned that John Healey’s budget at the end of October contains a £35 billion funding gap , leaving the government facing a choice between higher spending on public services or deep real-terms cuts later in the decade. In response, Green Party leader Zack Polanski said: “Economists are telling the Chancellor today that a steady-as-she-goes budget ‘is not an option’. With three in five families already cutting back on spending, and many about to see a £300 increase in their bills in January, we’re challenging the government to take bold and immediate action on the cost of living.
