Green Party leader Zack Polanski has demanded that Labour ‘comes clean’ on any impact assessments on the NHS from new drilling, after the Health Secretary, Yvette Cooper, told the BBC’s Laura Kuenssberg programme on Sunday that, “extreme heat has added to some of the pressure on the NHS”. The government is widely expected to give Jackdaw the green light on Thursday, having delayed the decision from September to after the Holborn and St Pancras byelection. Zack Polanski said: “Approval of new North Sea drilling wouldn’t take a penny off energy bills, according to the Energy Secretary herself, who has also said that, “to have a shot at staying within 1.5C global warming, we can’t have any new oil or gas fields!” I agree with the Energy Secretary and now we have the Health Secretary admitting that the summer heatwaves added to more pressure on an already under pressure NHS. “I am calling today on Labour to come clean and make available any impact assessments for the health service on their expected decision this week to approve new drilling in the North Sea. We know the heatwave placed great pressures on an NHS where much of its estate is not designed for extreme heat conditions alongside the health impacts of the weather, in particular on the elderly. If they haven’t done an impact assessment, they must delay any decision until this has been completed.” Further information Carla Denyer MP, the Green Party’s Spokesperson for Energy and Net Zero, has submitted a written Parliamentary question to the Secretary of State for Health and Social Care asking what discussions she has had with the Secretary of State for Energy Security and Net Zero about the estimated health impacts resulting from emissions linked to a potential decision to allow drilling at Rosebank oil field and Jackdaw gas field. Energy Secretary Miatta Fahnbulleh has previously opposed new oil and gas drilling: In 2022, she posted on X: To have a shot at staying within 1.5C global warming, we can’t have any new oil or gas fields. Banks know this, but some are pumping billions into new projects. It’s irresponsible and short sighted to be funding new #fossilfuels when we need to be getting to #NetZero fast. Then, in 2025 she said on X “I’ll say it slowly for those still clinging to the fossil fuel rollercoaster: Clean energy is the only way to lower bills- for good.” Oxfam analysis finds that if both the Rosebank and Jackdaw fields are approved, their combined lifetime emissions would contribute to an estimated 65,495 excess heat deaths – equivalent to wiping out the entire population of Clacton-on-Sea.
