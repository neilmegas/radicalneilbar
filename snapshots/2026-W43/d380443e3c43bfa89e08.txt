Anti-Capitalist Forum invites activists to Tampere on 14 November » Suomen kommunistinen puolue Menu Anti-Capitalist Forum invites activists to Tampere on 14 November English 21.10.2026 - 21:33 Suomeksi täällä! The Anti-capitalist Forum, convened by DSL Study Center , will be held at Kumppanuustalo Artteli (​Mustanlahdenkatu 22), Tampere, on Saturday, 14 November, from 11:00 to 17:00! This gathering is for anyone who opposes capitalism and wants to build a better world to replace it. The purpose of the Forum is to gain strength together, share experiences, learn from one another, strengthen collective action, and spend time in radical company. Register for the Forum here! Last registration day is 8 Nov. Due to meals, registration is binding. The forum will be held primarily in English. Participation in the Forum is free of charge. Preliminary program structure 11:00 Opening and orientation 11:30 Presentation and discussion 12:30 Lunch break 13:15 Workshops (two parallel sessions) 14:45 Break 15:00 Program and discussions 17:00 End of the Forum The program will be updated closer to the event. Changes are possible. The planning and preparations for the Forum have been carried out collectively by the organizing groups, developing our shared organizing skills along the way. Forum invites you to spend time in revolutionary company. Whether you are already involved in organized activism or just looking for your place, you are welcome to join. What matters most is the desire to dismantle capitalist oppression and imagine something better in its place. After the Forum, those interested can continue spending the evening together in Tampere on their own initiative. Forum is convened by DSL Study Center. Organizing groups include : Communist Party of Finland’s Tampere District VK-Tampere Students for Palestine Tampere Tampere with Palestine Radikaaliklubi Communist Party of Finland’s Oulu District TAIVO.
