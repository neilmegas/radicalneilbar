# RAPPORT 1.2 browser update

This package contains eight repository files plus this guide and stays below
GitHub's 100-file browser limit.

1. Unzip the package on your computer.
2. Open the extracted `RAPPORT-v1.2-browser-update` folder.
3. In GitHub, open the repository's **Code** page at its top level (where
   `run.py` is visible).
4. Choose **Add file → Upload files**.
5. Drag `run.py`, the `config` folder, and the `src` folder into the upload
   area. Do not upload the enclosing `RAPPORT-v1.2-browser-update` folder.
6. Commit the upload to `main`.
7. Open **Actions** and wait for **0 · Publish website** to finish successfully.
8. Hard-refresh the public site (`Ctrl+F5`, or `Cmd+Shift+R` on macOS).

The intermediate website may remain unchanged while the publishing action is
running. The final site should show **Website version 1.2**, 69 alphabetized
party cards with filters, and a dated political-power card on every party
profile.
